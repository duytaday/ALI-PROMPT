---
name: product-spec
description: Create or refine a PRD, user stories, acceptance criteria, scope, non-scope, metric, and release hypothesis. Use after discovery and before architecture or implementation. Do not decide technical design.
---

1. Ground the spec in the approved product vision and evidence.
2. Define persona, job-to-be-done, problem, desired outcome, and why now.
3. Specify scope, non-scope, user journey, policy decisions, and edge cases.
4. Write observable acceptance criteria with examples and anti-examples.
5. Define metric tree, primary success metric, guardrails, and instrumentation needs.
6. Identify dependencies, risks, support/operations impact, and open decisions.
7. Use `.ai/templates/PRD.md`.
8. Mark all unapproved business choices as `DECISION REQUIRED`.
